Craftsmanship

Built, not branded.

You pay for materials and engineering, never for a logo. That's the whole point.

01

Core

Built for performance, our paddles feature a premium polypropylene honeycomb core engineered to deliver the ideal balance of power, control, and consistency. The precision-built construction minimizes vibration for a smoother feel, while our reinforced edge guard is designed for long-lasting durability on and off the court.

02

Grip

Comfort matters. Our ergonomic handle is wrapped in a premium sweat-resistant, cushioned grip for a secure, confident hold in any condition. High-density foam inside the handle helps reduce vibration and improve comfort during long matches, while a reinforced end cap keeps everything locked in place.

03

Surface

Every paddle features a premium fiberglass face with a long-lasting textured finish designed to maximize spin, control, and responsiveness. The durable surface delivers consistent performance and exceptional feel, while our custom graphics are engineered to maintain their sharp appearance over time.
